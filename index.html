Luxury Tents for Resorts, Lodges, and Glamping

Safari Tents, also known as luxury tents, are mainly used as resort tents for overnight stays at resorts, hotels, and farmhouses and can be used for glamping in the deserts, hills, and jungles, as well as for jungle safaris. At Indian Tent Manufacturer, these luxury camping tents generally feature a verandah or a porch in the front, a bedroom in the middle, and a restroom at the back, making them ideal living tents for a rejuvenating weekend getaway.

Premium Materials for Comfort
and Durability

Our glamping safari tents and stunning bell tents are made of four layers of fabric. The outer fly is made of two layers- the upper one of rot-resistant and water-repellent cotton canvas in natural colour, whereas, in the inner fly, the upper cotton canvas comes with a lining of block-printed cotton sheeting. We also allow our customers to choose their inner prints and colours according to their requirements.

Porch and Outdoor Experience

The luxury camping tents offered by Indian Tent Manufacturer come with a porch in the front that is covered with mosquito-netted walls to keep flies and insects from getting inside your tent. You can enjoy your morning tea or candlelight dinner on the porch. We understand your requirements, thereby designing the event tents and the best safari tent in Australia for your needs.

Eco-Friendly Luxury and Customisation

Our canvas cabin tents provide immense luxury and are eco-friendly. You can choose from our wide range of luxury safari tents curated exclusively for glamping, hotels, resorts & tourism. They are exquisitely crafted with the best quality fabrics and sturdy structures for harsh weather conditions. The luxurious safari tents we have in our vault also feature all the basic amenities, such as fabric doors, sidewalls, windows, and whatnot. In addition, our exquisite safari tent glamping comes with a balcony and a wash area. Apart from this, we also add customised areas for a library, pantry, and play area for children, to name a few, as per your requirements.

Global Craftsmanship with a Touch of Elegance

We continuously aspire to showcase our superior and unmatched craftsmanship and quality of work in the global market, including Australia, the UK, the USA, and Europe. You will be thrilled to know that we also offer a connection of 2-4 cottage tents in Australia, transforming them into luxurious villas. So, if you are looking to place an order for canvas safari tents, then we are your sure-shot destination.
